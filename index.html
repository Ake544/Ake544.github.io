About Me

Building at the intersection of AI systems and real-world applications.

I'm a Full-Stack AI Engineer based in Ethiopia. I build end-to-end intelligent systems that combine machine learning, backend engineering, and modern application development to deliver production-ready AI products.

Over the past few years, I've built and deployed real-world AI systems including NLP chatbots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, computer vision models, and predictive analytics applications. My work spans both model development and full application engineering using Python, FastAPI, Flask, React, and React Native.

I have hands-on experience integrating LLMs, building semantic search systems with vector databases like FAISS, and deploying scalable backend services with authentication, databases, and cloud infrastructure. I've also worked on mobile-first AI applications with offline support and real-time features.

My engineering approach is simple: understand the problem deeply, build a working system quickly, then iterate toward scalability and production readiness.
